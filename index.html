QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I click Apply Now?

You will be redirected to the Gold Loan application page where you can provide the requested basic details.

Do I enter my personal details on this website?

No. This website does not contain a customer application form. When you choose to continue, you are redirected to the application page where the requested details can be provided.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee loan approval?

No. Submission of an enquiry does not guarantee loan approval. Eligibility and approval depend on the applicable lender's assessment, policies and terms.

Is the Gold Loan amount decided on this website?

No. The eligible loan amount and applicable terms are determined as part of the relevant loan assessment and process.

What happens after I submit my details?

After submitting the requested details on the application page, the enquiry can be taken forward through the applicable Gold Loan process.
